Formula Reference

Wavelength (λ = c / f)

The distance between successive peaks of an EM wave. At 2.4 GHz it’s ≈12.49 cm in free space.

Cutoff Frequency (fc = 1.8412 × c / (π × D))

Minimum frequency that propagates in a circular waveguide (TE11 dominant mode; 1.8412 is the first root of the Bessel derivative). Ensure fc < 2.4 GHz.

Guide Wavelength (λg = λ / √(1 − (fc/f)²))

Wavelength inside the guide; longer than free-space λ due to zig-zag propagation.

Probe Length (Lp = λ / 4)

Quarter-wave monopole fed by an N-type connector; copper wire ≈2–3 mm diameter works well.

Probe Offset (Dback = λg / 4)

Place the probe at a quarter guide wavelength from the back wall to hit an E-field maximum.

Rim Distance (Drim = L − Dback)

Distance from probe to the open end; larger values increase directivity but also size.

System Assumptions

  • N-type female on the back wall; 50 Ω system
  • TE11 mode; ideal conductor walls
  • Coax losses not modeled
