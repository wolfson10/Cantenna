Formulas

λ = c / f
Wavelength equals speed of light divided by frequency
fc = (1.8412 × c) / (π × D)
Cutoff frequency for the circular waveguide
λg = λ / √(1 − (fc / f)²)
Guide wavelength inside the can
Lp = λ / 4
Optimal probe length is quarter wavelength
Dback = λg / 4
Probe offset from the back of the can
Drim = L − Dback
Distance from probe to the open rim
