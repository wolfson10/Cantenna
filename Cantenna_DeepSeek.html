Reference Formulas

Wavelength: λ = c / f
Where c = 3×10⁸ m/s (speed of light), f = 2.4×10⁹ Hz
Cutoff Frequency: fc = (1.8412 × c) / (π × D)
Minimum frequency for TE₁₁ mode propagation in a circular waveguide (D in meters).
Guide Wavelength: λg = λ / √(1 − (fc/f)²)
Effective wavelength inside the waveguide (always longer than free-space λ).
Probe Length (Empirical): Lp = (λ/4) × k(D), with k(D) = clamp[0.85,0.97] (0.97 − 0.12·ln(D/7.33)), D in cm.
Quarter-wave monopole with logarithmic diameter correction. Small cans (≈7.3–8 cm) → k ≈ 0.95–0.97; large cans (≈15 cm) → k ≈ 0.88.
Probe Position: Dp = λg / 4
Quarter of the guide wavelength from the back wall to place the probe at the E-field maximum of the standing wave.
